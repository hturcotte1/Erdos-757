# Experiments

One folder per experiment: `experiments/<YYYYMMDD-HHMM>-<slug>/` with:
- `README.md`: question, method, exact command, parameters and seeds, result, conclusion, tags;
- the scripts used (or a pointer to e757/ functions);
- outputs: small ones in the folder; large ones gzipped under data/.

Long runs: `bash scripts/start_job.sh experiments/<id> <max_hours> <command...>`, then
`bash scripts/jobs.sh` to monitor. `run.log` and `job.pid` are git-ignored, so summarize the
results in README.md.

Special folders:
- `experiments/referee/`: the referee's independent checks.
- `experiments/falsify/`: falsifier reports (`gl-tight-structure`, `q8-abstract`, `q8-omega-inequality`, `extension-lemmas`, `deletion-lemma` for Lemma Delta', `i11-inequality` for the inequality I11, 11 tau <= 3n + 2m, and `l015-reader-family` for certificate L015, whose `REPORT.md` is the report).
  - `experiments/referee/L006-round1` to `L015-round3` and `C003-round1`, `C003-round2`: scratch folders of the referee rounds of the certificates L006 to L015 and of claim C003 (reviews: `results/reviews/L0NN-*__referee__*.md` and `C003-*__referee__*.md`, three per certificate and two for C003; the index in `referee/README.md` covers L001 to L015 and C003).
  - `experiments/prover/L007` to `L016`: the prover's reproduction folders of the certificates of the same id (`reproduce.sh` in each; `README.md` in `L007` and in `L013` to `L016`; large outputs are git-ignored, namely the `out/` directories and some `indep/` and `rerun/` files, see `.gitignore`). The certificate `L016` (the step n = 27, f(27) = 16) was submitted 2026-10-21 01:09 UTC and is unrefereed (referees running); its folder is complete. These folders are not experiments and have no row in the index below.
