## Summary

L017 is a pure-mathematics certificate: a structure theorem (Theorems 1-6) for a hypothetical minimal counterexample $H$ to I11 ($11\tau\le 3n+2m$) on the class $C$, and an unconditional theorem (Theorem 7) that I11 holds for every linear 3-graph of maximum degree $\le 2$. I read the whole certificate, numbered and re-derived every step of Steps 0-11 by hand, and re-did every computation that the certificate quotes with my own code (no import of `experiments/prover/L017`, `e757`, or the judge). Every mathematical step checks: the slack bookkeeping $s(H-v)=s+8-2d(v)$ (0.5 with 0.4), Lemma U'' in its one-, two- and $k$-vertex forms (2.1-2.2, including the isolated-vertex case), the Hall lemma (3.1-3.2: the vertex $x_e\in e\cap W_{\varphi(e)}$, the minimum transversal $T_{\varphi(e)}\ni x_e$ supplied by (U) because $F_i-x_e$ is a proper induced subgraph, the union $T$ covering all of $E^*$ including edges between two blocks, and $11|T_0|>\dots\ge 2|E^*|$ with $T_0=\emptyset$), the seven Hall-deficient patterns (my own enumeration of all surjective distributions of the six co-vertices over $r$ components gives exactly 1+5+1 patterns and none for $r\ge4$), the degree-2 and degree-1 statements, Theorem 7 (the identity $\tau(H)=m-\nu(G)$ needs no correction term; the Tutte-Berge argument $11\nu\ge3|E|$ for subcubic graphs is correct and strict for $|E|\ge1$; confirmed on all 1,842 subcubic graphs with at most 9 vertices and on all their dual 3-graphs), and the no-circularity claim (I11 is used only for proper induced subgraphs, except in Theorem 1(g) which correctly assumes $m(H)$ least). The cell lists of Theorem 6(i), 13.1, 13.3, 13.4, 13.5 and all 64 decompositions of C4 were reproduced exactly. The computational Corollaries 13.3 and 13.5 and Remarks 13.2, 13.4, 13.6 are clearly tagged "[verified-here as a computation, not refereed]" and conditional where stated; their pattern counts (roles) were recomputed independently on all 483 stored low-slack classes with an ILP-based method and agree class by class. The caveat that Theorems 1-5 do not use (P2)/(P3), so that $F_7$ and the two $(9,8,4)$ violators satisfy all their conclusions, is stated honestly (Step 10) and I confirmed it by brute force on those three graphs. The problems found are editorial only (one trivially-filled case omitted from the write-up of Theorem 4(vi) pattern (f), a timestamp, two wording points); no mathematical step is in doubt.

## Step-by-step audit of the proofs (hand verification)

Conventions and definitions. $s=3n+2m-11\tau$; minimal counterexample (fewest vertices in $C$) implies vertex-critical violator because proper induced subgraphs stay in $C$ (0.2) and have fewer vertices; $Z=V(H)$ gives the empty 3-graph with $s=0$. All minimal counterexamples have the same number of vertices (each excludes the other from having fewer), so "least $m$ among minimal counterexamples" in 1(g) is well defined. OK.

0.1-0.9. Additivity, heredity of (P1)-(P3) under vertex and edge deletion, $\tau(H)-|Z|\le\tau(H-Z)\le\tau(H)$, Fact 0.4 ($X$ co-transversal iff $\tau(H-X)=\tau(H)-|X|$; both directions checked, isolated vertices never co-transversal), 0.5 ($s(H-Z)=s-3|Z|-2|\partial Z|+11(\tau-\tau(H-Z))$, direct), 0.6 ($|\partial(x,y)|=d(x)+d(y)-\sigma$), 0.7 (every component of $H-Z$ meets an edge of $\partial Z$ when $H$ is connected; $u$ isolated in $H-v$ iff co-vertex of degree 1, which needs no isolated vertex in $H$, true where used). OK.

1.1-1.3 (R), (R+). The edge partition $E(R)\sqcup\bigsqcup E(F_i)\sqcup E^*$ is correct (an edge inside $W_i$ cannot be inside $W_j$). $T_R\cup T_0\cup\bigcup T_i$ is a transversal, hence the two inequalities. 1.3 needs only $Z\ne\emptyset$, resp. $W\neq\emptyset$ and all $W_i\ne V(H)$, so that $H-Z$, $R$, $F_i$ are proper induced subgraphs; the degenerate $E^*=\emptyset$ gives $0>s(R)+\sum s(F_i)\ge0$, i.e. it cannot occur, consistent with the statement of (H). OK.

2.1-2.2 (U). If $X$ is not co-transversal then $\tau(F-X)\ge\tau(F)-|X|+1$, so $s(F-X)\le s(F)+8|X|-11-2|\partial X|<0$ under the hypothesis, contradicting $s(F-X)\ge0$. One-vertex form $s(F)<3+2d(x)$; isolated $x$ with $s(F-x)\ge0$ forces $s(F)\ge3$ (also directly by additivity); two-vertex form $s(F)<2|\partial(x,y)|-5$; pair lemma is the contrapositive. The hypothesis "$s(F-X)\ge0$" is explicit in the statement. OK.

3.1-3.3 (H). Given injective $\varphi$ and $x_e\in e\cap W_{\varphi(e)}$ with $s(F_{\varphi(e)})<3+2d(x_e)$: $F_i-x_e$ is a proper induced subgraph of $H$, so (U) gives a minimum transversal $T_i\ni x_e$; injectivity means at most one prescribed vertex per block; every $e\in E^*$ (in particular every edge between two blocks, which is in $E^*$) contains its $x_e\in T_{\varphi(e)}$; so $T_0=\emptyset$ and 1.3 gives $0>s(R)+\sum s(F_i)+2|E^*|\ge0$. 3.2: $s(F_i)\le2$ forces no isolated vertex (2.2) and then $3+2d\ge5>s(F_i)$; likewise $s(F_i)\le4$ without isolated vertices. No circularity: all slacks assumed nonnegative are those of proper induced subgraphs. OK.

4.1-4.5 Theorem 7. $G$ simple (two degree-2 vertices in $e\cap f$ would violate linearity), subcubic, $|E(G)|=n_2$. Upper bound $\tau\le|M|+(m-2|M|)$. Lower bound: a minimum transversal $T$ has no isolated vertex; $|T|=|F|+|T_1|$, $|F|\ge|U|-k$, $k\le\nu$, and every $H$-edge not in $U$ is met by a degree-1 vertex of $T$, each covering one edge, so $|T_1|\ge m-|U|$; total $\ge m-\nu$. An edge all of whose vertices have degree 1 is an isolated vertex of $G$, counted correctly on both sides (contributes 1 to $m$, 0 to $\nu$, needs one transversal vertex): no correction term, confirmed by my brute force (and the sketch's correction term is indeed wrong at $E_1$, Step 11.4). 4.3: $3m=n_1+2n_2$ gives $s=3n_0+11\nu-3|E(G)|$. 4.4: Tutte-Berge $2\nu=N+|U|-o(G-U)$; $M\le3|U|+\sum e_i$; $2\nu-\frac6{11}M\ge\frac4{11}|U|+\sum t_i$ with $t_i=c_i-[c_i\ \mathrm{odd}]-\frac6{11}e_i$; I re-derived every case ($c=1$: 0; $c=2$: $\ge16/11$; $c=3$: $\ge4/11$; $c=4$: $\ge8/11$; $c=5$: $e\le7$, $\ge2/11$; even $c\ge6$: $\ge2c/11$; odd $c\ge7$: $e\le(3c-1)/2$, $\ge(2c-8)/11$). Equality only if $U=\emptyset$ and all $c_i=1$, i.e. $E(G)=\emptyset$; for $M\ge1$ strict, hence $11\nu-3M\ge1$. 4.5 conclusion and the connected case ($m\ge2$ connected forces a degree-2 vertex) OK. 4.6 is a remark (not used); the arithmetic $11(4n+4m+2)\le18(3n+2m)\iff 8m+22\le10n$ is right.
