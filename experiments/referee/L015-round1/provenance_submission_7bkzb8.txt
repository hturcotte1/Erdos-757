Two independent solvers for the Sidon numbers, K ≤ 8: OR-tools CP-SAT (optimal, bound = value), and a separately written integer construction with a distance-collision encoding on CaDiCaL with a cardinality constraint (≥ 7 + 5K satisfiable, ≥ 8 + 5K unsatisfiable). Already K = 6 is one 65-point set with Sidon number 37, and 37/65 < 4/7. Every depth, by proof. With L = 27169/251 the fixed point of φ, each point is uniquely φ^j(x), and 251(p − L) = (18/269)^j (251x − 27169), where 27169 = 101·269. A relation needs two points at its deepest level (mod 269) and is nonzero at its shallowest, and the sizes then bound its span by ten levels. Shifting levels preserves relations, so the depth-12 census covers every depth. The (4,5) property for all K and a four-state max-plus transfer matrix (vector [6,7,7,7] after copy 0, plus 5 per copy) follow. What it needed in addition was the fact that no relation crosses copies at any depth, which the level argument supplies. Novelty. Then S_K = O ∪ φ(O) ∪ … ∪ φᴷ(O) is a (4,5)-set with 11 + 9K points whose largest Sidon subset has 7 + 5K points, so c ≤ lim (7+5K)/(11+9K) = 5/9. (Scale by 269ᴷ for integers.) Why +5 per level: a Sidon set takes at most 6 of a new copy's 9 inner points, and at most 5 if it contains either anchor point of that copy (the images of 0 and 269).
