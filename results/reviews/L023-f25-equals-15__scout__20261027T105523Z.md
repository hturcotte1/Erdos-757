- Closest prior work (new to this scout's L023 baseline, but already known to the C008 scout): GitHub fortunto2/erdos-757-sidon45 (R. Salavatov, "SuperDuperAI", AI-agent-assisted, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23494596, CITATION.cff date-released 2026-10-21). Title: "Erdős Problem #757: no (4,5)-set below 4/7 on fewer than 25 points". Quote (README, Results 1): "No (4,5)-set has (n, h) with n = 16, h <= 9; n = 18, h <= 10; n = 20, h <= 11; n = 22, h <= 12; or n = 23, h <= 13 ... By Henning-Yeo these are the only shapes with n <= 24 that could beat 4/7, so every (4,5)-set with h/n < 4/7 has at least 25 points." Method: nauty chain enumeration of linear F7-free 3-graphs plus a rank test for realisability (n = 23: 86,094 classes, none realisable). It also gives a 14-point classification (every 14-point (4,5)-set with h = 8 has 12 APs, 9 hypergraph types). Overlap: this is the content of this project's K52-K55 and K58 (f(16), f(18), f(20), f(22), f(23)); our certificates L008-L011, L014 are dated 17-20 Oct 2026, the repo's release is 21 Oct 2026. So f(25) = 15 and the extension of the threshold from 25 to 29 are NOT anticipated. It is self-published and unrefereed; it is its own claim of "at least 25 points", which L023 sharpens to "at least 29 points" (hence the first possible record size is 29; C003's 65 points is separate).

### (2) The 8-point configuration Q_8 and its non-realizability: PARTIAL (the same 8-vertex piece and the same non-realizability conclusion were posted by a community claim on 24-25 Oct 2026; not in any refereed/published source; not in the F7 literature)
- Relation to the published F7 obstruction: Q_8 (two vertices a, b with three edges each whose six other vertices coincide; the link matchings form a hexagon) is the 6 lines of AG(2,3) through two points a, b other than line ab, on the 8 points other than the third point of line ab. It is F7-free as a subhypergraph (deleting any vertex leaves at most 4 edges; F7 has 5), so it is NOT excluded by the Gyarfas-Lehel/Henning-Yeo/Ma-Tang obstruction (F7 = AG(2,3) minus two points: 7 points, 5 edges). I found no published source (Gyarfas-Lehel 1995, Ma-Tang 2026, Henning-Yeo 2021 abstract, the a-Day report, kb/notes/) that lists Q_8 or any other realizability-based forbidden configuration beyond F7 (kb/notes/gyarfas_lehel_1995.md line 85 records the same: "Nothing beyond F7 is available as a forbidden configuration").
- Prior community posting: V3R1T4S PR #753 https://github.com/elder-plinius/V3R1T4S/pull/753 ("claim: erdos757-sidon-in-45-sets 2026-10-24-abshalom-dayan-6034e6cf", author avshalomd, branch dated 2026-10-24, description dated 25 Oct 2026, draft; "CONFIRMED_PARTIAL (p2)"). It exhibits a 101-vertex, 99-edge linear F7-free 3-graph with independence number 56 (56/101 < 5/9) that is explicitly NOT realizable, and says (quoted from the PR page via fetch): "H is not the A.P.-hypergraph of any (4,5)-set or weak Sidon set: an 8-vertex piece of every copy, the induced subhypergraph on {1,2,3,4,95,97,98,99} with edges (1,2,4), (1,3,98), (3,4,95), (2,95,97), (3,97,99), (2,98,99), forces two points to coincide or an extra 3-term progression for every allowed choice of middles." and "the reviewer checked it independently with exact rationals (all 95 distinct-middle choices forced degenerate)." I verified in this run (scratch script, e757.core.realize, exact generic-point test) that (i) this 8-vertex piece is isomorphic to Q_8 (degree-3 vertices 2 and 3, hexagon 1-4-95-97-99-98 on the six others) and (ii) of the 95 injective midpoint labellings of Q_8, 0 are realizable (matches L023 3.6: 35 NONINJ + 60 NOTWS). So the configuration and the count 95 are EXACTLY the same as L023's Proposition 6. The PR proves nothing beyond the 8-vertex computation (no runnable script: "The 'provably not realizable' clause has no runnable script in the package"), uses it only to say a 101-vertex abstract hypergraph is not an AP hypergraph, and names no AG(2,3) relation. It is unrefereed, a draft PR, and postdates this project's discovery only by the PR's own dates (L023 was written 27 Oct; the project's Q8 search is of the same day, so priority is not established either way; this is community material, not [published]).
- Consequence for L023: Proposition 6 should NOT be presented as the first appearance of the 8-vertex non-realizable piece; cite PR #753 as a community observation of the same configuration (counts agree). What appears new in L023 is: the identification with an AG(2,3) piece, the exact-solver proof from two programs sharing no code, the check that none of the 2,910 census patterns/A_base/the judged sets contain Q_8, and its use inside the f(25) argument (six classes of cell (25,23,11) each contain Q_8).
- PR #753 also contains the community remark "a chain family gives at most 6/11" for the abstract class (about independence ratio of non-realizable linear F7-free graphs), consistent with this project's I11 / 6/11 discussion (K60 context); not about L023.
