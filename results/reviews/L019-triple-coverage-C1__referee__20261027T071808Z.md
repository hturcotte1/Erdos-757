**Theorem 5 (folding lemma), re-derived.** (a) $y,z$ non-adjacent, so every image edge has 3 vertices; $\varphi(g)=\varphi(h)$ with $g\ne h$ forces $\zeta$ in the image with $y\in g$, $z\in h$ and $g\setminus\{y\}=h\setminus\{z\}$, two common vertices, impossible in a linear 3-graph; hence $m(K^\circ)=m-2$ exactly (only the two edges at $x$ disappear), $n(K^\circ)=n-2$, $d(\zeta)=d(y)+d(z)-2$, other degrees do not increase. (b) Lift $T^\circ$: replace $\zeta$ by $\{y,z\}$, or add $x$ if $\zeta\notin T^\circ$; both edges at $x$ are met; size $+1$. (c) $x\in T\in\mathcal T(K)$: $\varphi(T\setminus\{x\})$ meets every $\varphi(g)$, so $\tau(K^\circ)\le\tau-1$, equality by (b); $s(K^\circ)=3(n-2)+2(m-2)-11(\tau-1)=s-6-4+11=s+1$. (d) Non-linearity of $K^\circ$ needs two images sharing $\zeta$ and a vertex $t\notin\{y,z\}$, one preimage through $y$ and one through $z$, i.e. a common neighbour $t$ of $y,z$ in $K-x$ (the common neighbour $x$ of $K$ is correctly excluded by "in $K-x$"); conversely such $t$ gives two distinct images sharing $\{\zeta,t\}$. An $F_7$ avoiding $\zeta$ is an $F_7$ of $K$. For $S^\circ\ni\zeta$, $S=\varphi^{-1}(S^\circ)$ has $|S|=|S^\circ|+1$ and $e_{K^\circ}(S^\circ)=e_K(S)$ by injectivity, so (P3) fails at $S^\circ$ iff $e_K(S)=|S|-2$; $|S|\in\{3,4,5\}$ is impossible for a saturated set containing two non-adjacent vertices (an edge through $y,z$; two edges in 4 points; three edges in 5 points).
