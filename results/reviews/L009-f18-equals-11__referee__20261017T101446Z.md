## Summary

L009 claims three things:
- every H in C (linear, F7-free, e(S) <= |S|-2) with 18 vertices has tau(H) <= 7, so every 18-point (4,5)-set has h >= 11;
- the explicit set A_18 has h = 11, hence f(18) = 11;
- 11 tau <= 3n + 2m holds on C for every n <= 18.

The argument is the L007/L008 induction:
- Lemma 17 empties the only n = 17 window cell, (17,12,7).
- I11 at n-1 gives Delta <= 3 in the cells (18,11,7) and (18,16,8).
- In (18,16,8), deleting a degree-3 vertex leaves a slack-0 graph: either a connected (17,13,7) graph T, or a connected (14,12,6) graph T plus a disjoint edge.
- Lemma R lists every such T by re-inserting a degree-3 vertex into slack-2 graphs.
- A final one-vertex extension shows that no tau = 8 graph exists.

I re-derived every step by hand and with my own arithmetic script. I read the prover's programs. I re-ran the prover's whole labelled pipeline C2 to C7: every load-bearing count was reproduced, and the SHA-256 of all 23 regenerated files matched the recorded sums.

I re-did each load-bearing step with my own code:
- nauty 2.8.8 genbg with hooks I wrote myself (an exact tau prune, an optional F7 prune, and output tests for tau, F7 and (P3));
- a brute-force generator with no symmetry breaking;
- a definitional C-membership checker (networkx subgraph monomorphism for F7, all vertex subsets for (P3));
- a standalone C filter;
- my own extension program with an exact tau computation.

Three complete direct enumerations confirm both tight lists of Lemma R. None of them uses Lemma R, the component lists or the decomposition:
- (14,12,tau>=6), Delta <= 3: 38 classes, 2 in C;
- (17,13,tau>=7), Delta <= 3, tau prune only: 80 classes, 6 in C;
- (17,13,tau>=7), Delta <= 3, F7-pruned: exactly the 6 F7-free classes, in a split run and in an unsplit run.

These are exactly the classes of the prover's T14_conn and T17_conn lists, and the classes of my own class-level rebuild. Further checks:
- My own final extension of the 6 + 2 tight classes gives 43,224 extensions, all with tau = 7.
- On all 118 unfiltered tight classes it gives 365,194 extensions; the 9 with tau = 8 all contain F7 and violate (P3).
- Lemma 17 holds directly: 1 class, which contains F7.
- The empty cells are empty.
- A_18 is a (4,5)-set with h = 11 (exhaustive search, CP-SAT, and the judge).
- About 333,000 AP hypergraphs of real (4,5)-sets satisfy I11; none with 18 points has tau >= 8.

No mathematical or computational problem remains. ACCEPT.
