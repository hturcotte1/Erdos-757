## The argument, re-derived (steps of the proof)

1. **Step 0 and the classes.**
   - For a (4,5)-set, x+y = z+w with distinct pairs gives a 4-set with two coincidences among its 6 differences, hence at most 4 distinct distances. So A is weak Sidon, and Sidon subsets are exactly the 3-AP-free subsets: h = α = n − τ.
   - Distinct APs have distinct midpoints, never min S or max S, so e(S) ≤ |S|−2. This gives (P3).
   - (P1) and (P2) are K6 and K7.
   - F7 is maximal-linear on its 7 points: I checked that each of the 6 uncovered pairs fails to extend to a linear triple. So "contains F7" is the same notion whether or not copies are required to be induced.
2. **Step 1, window cells.**
   - Recomputed by hand and by `src/cells.py`: (5,3,2) (8,4,3) (10,6,4) (11,5,4) (12,9,5) (13,7,5) (14,11,6) (15,9,6) (15,10,6) (16,8,6) (16,13,7) (16,14,7).
   - Row n = 16: lo/hy = 6/6, 7/6, 7/6, 7/6, 7/6, 7/7, 7/7 for m = 8..14.
   - `decomp.py 16` output is identical to the stored file.
3. **Step 2, Lemma A′.** Correct.
   - A negative-slack component C ∈ O+ lies in a window cell: τ(C) ≥ lo; τ(C) ≤ hy by K8 (C is linear and F7-free); τ ≤ m; m ≤ n−2 by (P3); n ≤ 2m+1 by connectivity.
   - n_C ≤ 13 is excluded by L007 Lemma A, n_C = 14 by L007 Theorem 1, and n_C = 15 by the two empty cells.
   - I re-checked all of these independently (item G below).
4. **Step 3, Lemma B′.**
   - m ≤ 14, so 17τ ≤ 122, so τ = 7 and m ≥ 13.
   - For every v: 66 ≤ 11τ(H−v) ≤ 45 + 2(m−d(v)), so d(v) ≤ m−11, and τ(H−v) = 6 because 77 > 73.
   - m = 13 would need 39 ≤ 32, which is false. So m = 14, d ≤ 3, and d₃ ≥ 10.
   - Without K8: 3m ≤ 16(m−11) forces m = 14, and then τ(H) ≤ τ(H−v)+1 ≤ 7 (checked by script).
5. **Step 4, Lemma C′.**
   - s(G) = 1, and component slacks are integers ≥ 0, so there is no isolated vertex.
   - My congruence tables and `src/cells.py` (which tries every τ ≤ m, without HY) give: slack 0: (3,1,1), (10,7,4), (13,8,5), (14,12,6); slack 1: (8,5,3), (11,6,4), (12,10,5), (14,7,5), (15,11,6).
   - An exhaustive multiset search, isolated vertices included, gives exactly [(15,11,6)] and [(3,1,1),(12,10,5)].
   - Δ(G) ≤ 3 follows from Δ(H) ≤ 3.
6. **Step 5, extension.**
   - The three pairs are pairwise disjoint (two edges at v share only v) and none lies inside a G-edge (linearity).
   - Each endpoint lies in exactly one v-edge, so its G-degree is ≤ 2.
   - The isomorphism transfer to a labelled representative is valid, and (P2) and (P3) are isomorphism invariants.
   - Hit criterion: τ(H) ≤ 6 iff some 6-transversal of G meets all three pairs, since a ≤6-transversal containing v would leave a 5-transversal of G. This is correct; I used an independent τ test anyway.
7. **Steps 6–7.**
   - Corollary 3 is correct; (16,8,6) is empty.
   - Theorem 2 follows from Theorem 1 with K3–K7.
   - f is monotone.
   - Corollary 4: recomputing the HY feasibility for n ≤ 30 gives 16, 18, 20, 22, 23, 25, 27, 29, 30; removing 16 leaves the stated list.
8. **No hidden assumption.**
   - Connectivity of H is never assumed; it follows anyway from s(H) = −1.
   - No [community] fact is used, nor K30/K31; nothing assumes midpoint saturation.
   - There is no circularity: Theorem 1 uses Lemma A′ only for n ≤ 15.
