## Summary

I re-derived every step of the argument by hand. Every computation was re-run, and each load-bearing computation was redone with a different method and code I wrote myself. The steps re-derived are: the window cells at n = 14, 15, 16; the use of Henning–Yeo; the degree bound and the exclusion of m = 13; the slack-0 and slack-1 cell lists; the decomposition of a 15-vertex remainder; the extension step; the two filters; and the passage from (4,5)-sets to the class O ⊆ O+.

The independent checks were these.
- **Enumerations.** Both enumerations of the proof, C2 (connected linear (12,10,τ≥5), Δ≤3) and C3 ((15,11,τ≥6), Δ≤3), were redone by orderly generation. The tool was nauty's `genbg` (McKay's canonical augmentation, nauty 2.8.8), with a τ-prune hook I wrote. This method has nothing in common with the canonical BFS-growth process of L007 Lemma D, which both `enum_conn` and `conn_dfs3` implement. It returns exactly the same isomorphism classes: 197 for C2 and 49 for C3, compared by nauty canonical forms.
- **Brute-force validation.** All three generators (`genbg`+hook, `enum_conn`, `conn_dfs3`) match a labelled brute force with no symmetry reduction on every cell with n ≤ 9 (any maximum degree) and n = 10 (Δ ≤ 3). That is 271 cells, with no mismatch.
- **Extension and filters.** I wrote the extension step again with a definitional τ test, and the filters with a definitional F7 test (all 7! bijections) and a P3 test over all 2^16 vertex subsets. Applied to the genbg class representatives, this pipeline reproduces the falsifier's class-level counts exactly (case B 154,309 extensions and 218 hits; case A, on the 149 O+ classes, 74,916 and 18). It leaves 0 survivors in both cases. The hits fall into the same isomorphism classes (11 and 18) as the authors' labelled-level pipeline.
- **Lemma A′.** I re-checked it independently on all window cells with n ≤ 15 using genbg. The cell (14,11,≥6) has 3 connected classes without a degree bound, and all 3 contain F7; every other window cell is empty.
- **Route without Step 4.** As an extra check, I bypassed the component decomposition of Step 4 entirely. I enumerated all 261 classes of linear (15,11,τ≥6,Δ≤3) 3-graphs, of any connectivity and with isolated vertices allowed, and extended them: 609 hits, all violating (P3), 0 survivors.
- **Without Henning–Yeo.** Further genbg runs show that Lemma A′, and with it Theorems 1 and 2, also hold without Henning–Yeo (Problems, item 4).
- **Data and witness.** Every lemma holds on all realizable data I tested. C16 is a (4,5)-set with h = 10, by brute force, by CP-SAT and by the frozen judge.

I found no mathematical or computational error. The remarks below are editorial or optional.

## Enumerator audit (completeness)

- **`enum_conn.c` (prover).** Read line by line. Phase A takes {c,a,b} with c<a<b<vstart in strict lexicographic order. Phase B takes {c,a,new} with c<a<vstart in increasing order. Phase C takes {c,new,new'}. The turn closes iff c+1 < nv. The prunes are nv+2r ≥ N, τ(P) ≥ T0−r (exact branching hitting set), degree ≤ DMAX and pair-freeness. It is exactly the canonical process of L007 Lemma D, whose proof I re-derived (D1–D5). Every connected linear graph with the parameters has a surviving canonical branch, and every output is valid.
- **`conn_dfs3.c` (falsifier).** `diff` against `conn_dfs2_all.c` shows only the added CHK/SLICE wrappers. With arguments `0 0` and K = 1 both are identically true, so `conn_dfs3` is `conn_dfs2_all`.
- **Slicing rule.** Correct, and verified empirically.
  - (12,10,5): slices of 73,902 + 71,131, disjoint, both with cnt = 20,543, union equal to the unsplit run.
  - (15,11,6): the falsifier's stored slices of 26,041 + 24,565 are disjoint, both have cnt = 21,698, and their union equals my re-run of the single-process `enum_conn` as labelled sets.
- **Brute force (mine, `bin/bf_label.c`).** All labelled linear edge sets on [n], each exactly once, with Δ ≤ D. It tallies the connected spanning ones by (m, τ), with τ computed by subset enumeration. Sanity values: 30 labelled Fano planes and 420 = 7!/12 labelled F7 at n = 7. `scripts/validate_small.py` compares, for every cell and every threshold t = 0..τmax+1, the brute-force labelled count with Σ n!/|Aut| over the classes output by (i) genbg+hook, (ii) `enum_conn`, (iii) `conn_dfs3`, with classes and |Aut| from nauty. It also checks that the three class sets are identical.
  - n = 3..9, D ∈ {3, ∞}: 235 cells, 164 non-empty, 0 mismatches.
  - n = 10, D = 3: 36 cells, 28 non-empty, 0 mismatches (brute force 18 min).

## Problems found (in order of severity)

No mathematical or computational problem. All items are editorial or optional.

1. **Attribution of the cells (15,9,6) and (15,10,6) (DEPENDS ON, Step 2.4).** These are load-bearing here, and the proof cites them as "L007 computation C6". L007 itself labels C6 "Remark cells (Step 9.1; not load-bearing)" and says Step 9 is "NOT certified here". They were in fact re-checked by all three L007 referees with their own enumerators, and here with genbg (empty, no degree bound), so nothing is missing. The DEPENDS ON line should say so rather than present them as part of L007's accepted content.
2. **Miscount in Step 4.3.** "All eight cells also satisfy τ ≤ m and K8" lists nine cells: the five slack-1 cells, (14,12,6), and L007's three slack-0 cells.
3. **Method independence.** The header's "two independently written programs" is true, but `enum_conn` and `conn_dfs3` implement the same canonical process (Lemma D), as the falsifier report notes. This review supplies the methodologically independent orderly generation (nauty genbg) that the falsifier asked for; the authors may cite it.
4. **Role of Henning–Yeo (K8).** K8 is [published] via Ma–Tang Thm 5.3, and its hypotheses (3-uniform, linear, F7-free) hold wherever it is applied; the project has not seen the full text. As noted in step 3 above, Step 3.1 does not actually need K8 once Lemma A′ is known: the degree count alone forces m = 14 and τ = 7. So K8 enters only through the window-cell reductions of L007 Lemma A and Lemma A′. Optional strengthening, not required:
   - **n ≤ 13.** L007 round 1 showed Lemma A without K8.
   - **n = 14, 15.** I checked with genbg the extra cells that would have to be checked without K8: (14,7..10,≥6), (14,12,≥7), (15,7,≥6), (15,8,≥6), (15,11,≥7), (15,12,≥7). All contain no connected linear 3-graph at all.
   - **(15,13,≥7).** Also empty: three genbg slices `0/3`, `1/3`, `2/3`, about 407 CPU-s each, 0 outputs.
   - **n ≤ 13, re-checked.** I re-ran all 35 cells with genbg: the only connected linear 3-graph with 11τ > 3n+2m and m ≤ n−2 is F7 itself, at (7,5,3).
   - **Consequence.** Lemma A′ holds on O+ without K8. The slack lists of Step 4 are the same without K8, and Step 3 does not need it. So Theorems 1 and 2 hold without Henning–Yeo, by the referee computations (`out/hyfree_*`). The authors may add this; it removes the only dependence on a paper whose full text the project has not seen.
5. **Optional strengthening.** The component decomposition of Step 4 can be bypassed computationally (the "route without Step 4" row: all 261 classes, 0 survivors). This gives a second derivation of Theorem 1 from Step 3 alone.
6. **Housekeeping (mine, fixed).** Because of a wrong working directory, a backgrounded command of this review wrote a 109-byte timing/error file `extvertex2_B.time` into the repository root (the command itself failed immediately and was re-run correctly). I deleted it at once; no other file outside `experiments/referee/L008-round1/` and this review was created or changed.
