- **Independent generation (mine).** `bin/genbg_r3` = nauty 2.8.8 `genbg` with my `src/r3_hooks.c`: PRUNE1 rejects an intermediate graph with $k$ edges if $\tau<T_0-(\mathrm{maxn2}-k)$ (hereditary: removing an edge lowers $\tau$ by at most 1), optionally if it contains $F_7$ (definitional test) or violates (P3) (both monotone under adding edges); so every output graph keeps its whole canonical ancestor chain.
