Why H.1 applies: L021 8.1 is proved for every family X with |E*(X)| = |X|+1 in a minimal counterexample; its proof uses only Claim (E) for the nonempty subfamilies S of X (so that |E*_H(S)| = |N_B(S)| >= |S|+1 and B-e has a perfect matching) and (L1) for the pieces (0.6).
