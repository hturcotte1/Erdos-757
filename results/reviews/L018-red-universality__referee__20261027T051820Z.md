## L017 / L018 reconciliation (Theorem 4 of L017 versus Theorem 6(b') of L018)

Both are correct, and L018's statement is strictly stronger; L018 uses no extra hypothesis. L017 Theorem 4(iii) lists the distributions of the six co-vertices over the components of H - v that are Hall-deficient (no matching saturating the three pairs): seven patterns, (a) r = 1; (b), (c1), (c2), (e), (f) with r = 2; (d) with r = 3; and Theorem 4(iv) gives, for (c1), (c2), (d), the condition equivalent to tau(H) = tau(G) + 1: "no minimum transversal of F_1 meets both P_1 and P_2". L018 Step 6.5' proves that this condition is impossible for a minimal counterexample: the induced subgraph H_1 = F_1 plus v with the two edges e_1, e_2 is a proper induced member of C, so I11 holds for it, while the condition forces tau(H_1) = tau(F_1) + 1 and slack(H_1) = slack(F_1) - 4 < 0. Hence L017's patterns (c1), (c2), (d) are void, which is exactly L018's "no component contains two pairs; r <= 2". The surviving patterns coincide: L017 (a), (b), (e), (f) = L018's connected case and the types {s,s,s}, {1,2,s}, {1,s,s} of 6.7/8.6. L017's statement remains true as written (it only says which distributions are Hall-deficient, and "r <= 3"), but three of its seven patterns cannot occur. Lemma M: L017 Theorem 7 (Tutte-Berge, 11 nu >= 3|E|) and L018 Theorem 2 (Edmonds, 7 nu >= 2|E|) are both correct; L018's constant is better and attained.
