Justification of the method (in the docstring of `dp2.py`). Fix a vertex order; after processing the first i vertices, the frontier F_i is the set of processed vertices that still share an AP with an unprocessed vertex. Every AP whose last vertex is processed at step i has its two other vertices in F_{i−1}, so whether the i-th vertex may be added to an independent set is decided by the chosen-pattern on the frontier alone; vertices leaving the frontier appear in no later AP, so only the best size per remaining pattern is kept. By induction the table after step i holds, for every achievable pattern on F_i, the maximum size of an AP-free subset of the processed vertices with that pattern; the final maximum is h. The sorted order has frontier 38 (the copies of the base family nest towards the centre; my first attempt with it was OOM-killed at 13.9 GB, which is a cost problem of that order, not a correctness problem); the "both ends inward" order has frontier ≤ 10.
