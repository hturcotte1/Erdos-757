- (b) Independence. Checked the matrix argument: N[e][f] = coefficient of X_{mu(f)} in l_e is -2 on the diagonal, +1 at e^- (mu(e^-) = a(e)) and e^+ (mu(e^+) = c(e)), 0 otherwise; injectivity of mu makes e^-, e^+ unique. So N is nonsingular, hence the m forms restricted to the m midpoint columns are independent, hence the forms are independent. Uses only injective midpoints and a < mu < c. OK.
