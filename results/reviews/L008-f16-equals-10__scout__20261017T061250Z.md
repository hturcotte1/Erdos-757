2. The tool, 11 tau <= 3n + 2m on linear F7-free 3-graphs with e(S) <= |S|-2: not found in any accessible source (see the L007 scout, results/reviews/L007-no-nonsaturated-extremal-14-point-sets__scout__20261017T034349Z.md, whose search I repeated in part). Only the proved range n <= 16 is claimed by L008, and it is computer-assisted and project-internal.
