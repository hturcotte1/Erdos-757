Function: `reflib.forbidden_sets`. For every sum value it collects all pairs x ≤ y with that sum, and forms the union of every two distinct pairs. This does not assume weak Sidon.

This gives a second, structural confirmation of the (4,5) property. In a 4-set, a coincidence of differences comes either from a 3-AP inside it or from a relation a+d = b+c among 4 distinct points, and the latter forces two coincidences. So the (4,5) property is equivalent to being weak Sidon with a linear AP hypergraph. Under these conditions, the number of 4-sets with exactly 5 distances must be m(n−3) = 55·55 = 3,025, and that is the count observed.
