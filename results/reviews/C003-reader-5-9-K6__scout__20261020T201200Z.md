One discrepancy worth recording (from our own rebuild, experiments/20261020-1730-reader-5-9/README.md and cpsat_K8_long.json): the page says CP-SAT was "optimal, bound = value" for K <= 8. We could not reproduce that for a plain CP-SAT model (optimal only for K <= 3 within 240 s; with our added per-copy cuts optimal for K <= 7; K = 8 still FEASIBLE 47 with bound 55 after 3000 s). This does not contradict the claim: h(S_8) = 47 was confirmed by our frontier DP and by SAT >= 47 / UNSAT >= 48. It only means the page's CP-SAT optimality statement for K = 7, 8 depends on a model or run we cannot see.
