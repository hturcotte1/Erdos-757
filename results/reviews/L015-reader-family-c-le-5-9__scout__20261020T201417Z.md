## C. Is the page's every-depth proof the same as L015's?
Page (and submission page) describe: write p = phi^j(x), level j, fixed point L = 27169/251, 251(p-L) = (18/269)^j (251x - 27169), 27169 = 101*269. "A relation must balance its deepest level modulo 269, which needs two points there; it must be nonzero at its shallowest level; and the sizes then force the span between the deepest and shallowest levels to be at most ten." Then "Shifting levels preserves relations, so every relation at any depth is a shifted copy of one already in S_12" (depth-12 census: exactly 9*13 = 117 relations, all inside one copy; referee brute-forced to depth 150). The (4,5) step: "two progressions on four points would have to sit in consecutive copies through both shared points, and no progression of O contains both 0 and 269". Sidon number: four-state max-plus transfer matrix, [6,7,7,7] then +5.
L015: same level formula and same mod-269 starting identity (Steps 1-2). Differences:
- L015 proves, by a complete case analysis on the number m of positions at the deepest level (m=1,2 impossible by F5(i)-(iii); m=3 forces the two relations at anchors 0/269 via F5(iv); m=4 reduces to O), that EVERY relation is already the image of a relation of O under some phi^J. No span bound ("at most ten"), no shifting argument and no depth-12 census are used (L015 Step 8.1). The page's claim "needs two points at the deepest level" corresponds to m >= 2, whereas L015 shows m=3 is the only cross-level case and lands on anchors; the page's span-ten bound is therefore a weaker intermediate statement (L015: a posteriori span <= 1).
- L015 adds: proof that Sidon subsets of S_K are exactly consistent tuples of per-copy admissible sets (5.3 bijection), hand proofs of the six table inequalities (5.6) so the upper bound h <= 7+5K needs no computer for the table, and an explicit Sidon witness T_K of size 7+5K for all K (Step 6), plus the formulation-independent step 7.4 (supremum form, no Ma-Tang K1).
- Same in substance: level/fixed-point identity, "(4,5) via linearity of the AP hypergraph and anchors 0,269 not in one AP", transfer matrix [6,7,7,7] (+5).
- Caveat: the page's full proof file was not retrievable, so "different" is judged from the page's own 3-sentence summary, which is explicit about the span bound and census. The repository's earlier experiment README (experiments/20261020-1730-reader-5-9/README.md section 5) already follows the deepest-level route; this scout did not re-check that file's content.
