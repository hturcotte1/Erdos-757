## Definitions used (kb/00_START_HERE.md section 1)

- (4,5)-set: every 4 distinct points determine at least 5 distinct absolute differences; equivalently
  |B - B| >= 11 for every 4-subset B (B - B contains 0 and both signs, so |B-B| = 1 + 2 * #distances).
- S is Sidon: all positive differences y - x (x < y in S) are distinct; equivalently, all sums x + y with
  x <= y are distinct. h(A) is the size of the largest Sidon subset. f(n) is min h(A) over n-point
  (4,5)-sets.
