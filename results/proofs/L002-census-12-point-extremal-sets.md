## Statement
Up to affine equivalence (translation, scaling, reflection) there are exactly 54,243 sets $A\subset\mathbb{R}$ with $|A|=12$ that are (4,5)-sets with largest Sidon subset of size $h(A)=7=f(12)$. Each has exactly $m=10$ three-term APs and is affinely rigid (determined by its AP pattern).

## Proof
1. (Reduction to patterns.) For a 12-point (4,5)-set with $h=7$: K5 gives $m\le10$, and the enumeration below shows directly that no class-O pattern on 12 vertices with $m\le9$ has $\alpha\le7$ (so Henning–Yeo is not needed). Its AP pattern in rank order is an ordered midpoint hypergraph that is linear (K6), $F_7$-free (K7), has injective midpoints (K5) and satisfies K30. Class O := all such patterns.
2. (Enumeration.) The exact C DFS `e757/c/ordered_dfs` enumerates every pattern of class O on 12 vertices with exactly $m$ edges and $\alpha\le7$: for $m=10$ it finds 2,226,490 labelled patterns (187M nodes, 288 s), 1,113,245 up to reflection; for $m=9$ it finds 0 patterns (425,209,182 nodes, 607 s). For $m\le8$ the census engine was not run; the absence of patterns there follows from Henning–Yeo (K8) and was also verified by the referees' independent enumerators (experiments/referee/L002/n12/mle8_runs.log; experiments/referee/L002-round2/enum_n12_noneall.log, which imposes no F7 filter and no bound on the number of non-midpoints). Engine: e757/c/ordered_dfs.c, census produced with the ea6c3d9 source and reproduced identically at commit d2f70f5 (m=10: 187,004,404 nodes). Consistency check (containment only, not completeness): all 30,559 patterns found by the unfinished CP-SAT enumeration (experiments/20261014-0610-extremal-census) occur in the DFS output. Completeness was confirmed by the referee's independent enumerator (different search design, identical 2,226,490 patterns; a run without alpha-pruning over all 686,700,420 saturated linear K30 patterns gives the same per-split counts).
3. (Exact realization.) Pipeline: 754,235 of the 1,113,245 patterns are free of medial triangles (K31) and 395,039 of those avoid every forbidden core with at most 4 edges; the 718,206 discarded patterns are unrealizable because K31 and each core are proved forced relations (K31-K33). Every one of the 395,039 remaining patterns is decided by the pipeline's exact realizer `experiments/20261014-1005-census-dfs/fastreal.py` (exact rational arithmetic; the fallback to `e757.realizability.realize_exact` was never needed, see experiments/20261014-1005-census-dfs/results/realize_summary.json): 54,243 are realizable, each realization checked to be a (4,5)-set with exactly this AP pattern and $h=7$; the rest fail exactly (321,304 order-infeasible, 10,803 forced extra AP, 8,689 forced repeated difference). Independent confirmation without the filters: referee round 1 realized all 2,226,490 labelled patterns with its own integer solver and found exactly 108,486 realizable ones, i.e. the same 54,243 reflection pairs; referee round 2 did the same with a from-scratch enumerator and realizer over all 2,678,082 patterns including the 451,592 containing $F_7$ (all unrealizable), with exact integer certificates. Rigidity: for a saturated pattern the AP system (with $x_0=0$, $x_{11}=1$) has matrix $2(I-B)$ with $B$ substochastic and every vertex reaching a deficient row by following left endpoints down to vertex 0, so $\rho(B)<1$ and the system is nonsingular (0 singular systems among the 2,226,490 patterns).
4. (Counting classes.) Realizable patterns are affine invariants of their (rigid) realizations, and reflection has been quotiented out, so the 54,243 realizable patterns are in bijection with the affine classes of 12-point (4,5)-sets with $h=7$. The three previously known inequivalent 12-point sets (data/small_records.json '12' and the two omega sets in e757/core.py KNOWN; five names in the knowledge base for three sets) are among the classes. The frozen judge passed 50 random sets (PASS NO-IMPROVEMENT, h=7).
