## Statement

$C$ is the class of L007–L009: 3-graphs that are (P1) linear, (P2) $F_7$-free and (P3) satisfy $e(S)\le|S|-2$ for every vertex set with $|S|\ge2$; $s(H)=3n+2m-11\tau(H)$.

**Theorem 1 (I11 up to 20).** Every $H\in C$ with $n\le20$ satisfies $11\,\tau(H)\le3n+2m$. In particular, for $n=19$: $\tau\le\lfloor(57+2m)/11\rfloor$, and for $n=20$: $\tau\le\lfloor(60+2m)/11\rfloor$.

**Theorem 2.** Every 20-point (4,5)-set has $h\ge12$, and $C_{20}=A_{\rm base}\cup\{5000,5101,5202\}\cup\{20000,20103,20206\}$ is a 20-point (4,5)-set with $h=12$. Hence $f(20)=12$, and no 20-point (4,5)-set beats $4/7$ (that needs $h\le\lceil80/7\rceil-1=11$, $\tau=9$, $m=18$; kb/00_START_HERE.md §4).

**Remark 3 (what Theorem 1 says about $n=19,21,22,23$).**
(a) $n=19$: Theorem 1 gives $\tau\le\lfloor(57+2\cdot17)/11\rfloor=8$, i.e. $h\ge11$, which is already implied by $f(19)\ge f(18)=11$ (L009). At $\tau=8$ Henning–Yeo already needs $m\ge14$, and Theorem 1 excludes $m=14,15$ (the cells (19,14,8), (19,15,8)); a 19-point set with $h=11$ and $m\in\{16,17\}$ (I11 slack 1 and 3) is not excluded. So $f(19)\in\{11,12\}$ stays open.
(b) $n=21$: no 21-point set can beat $4/7$ by Henning–Yeo alone (it would need $\tau=10$ and $m\ge22>19$). $n=22$: a record needs $(n,m,\tau)=(22,20,10)$, slack $-4$; with I11 at $n=21$ its degree bound would be $\lfloor(-4+8)/2\rfloor=2$, and $3m=60>2\cdot22$, so **I11 at $n=21$ alone would close $n=22$**; that needs the four window cells (21,11,8), (21,12,8), (21,16,9), (21,17,9) (slacks −3, −1, −4, −2). $n=23$: a record needs $\tau=10$, $m\in\{19,20,21\}$ (Henning–Yeo); I11 excludes $m=19,20$ but **not** $m=21$: $(23,21,10)$ has slack $69+42-110=1\ge0$. So I11 cannot exclude a 23-point record (`cells_21_23.py`).

## Proof

### Step 0. Facts used
0.1 (L007–L009 Step 0.) Additivity of $n,m,\tau,s$ over components; a single vertex has $s=3$; heredity of $C$; for $H\in C$: $\tau\le m$, $\tau(H)-1\le\tau(H-v)\le\tau(H)$, $m\le n-2$, $17\tau\le5n+3m$ (K8); a connected 3-graph with $m\ge1$ edges has $n\le2m+1$; deletion does not increase degrees.
0.2 I11 holds on $C$ for $n\le18$ (L009 Theorem 3, which uses L007 and L008).
0.3 (Component argument, L009 0.3.) If I11 holds on $C$ below $N$ vertices, every $H\in C$ with $N$ vertices and $s(H)<0$ is connected.
0.4 (Degree lemma.) If I11 holds on $C$ below $N$ vertices and $H\in C$ has $N$ vertices, $m$ edges, $\tau(H)=\tau$ and $s=s(H)<0$, then for every vertex $v$: $\tau(H-v)=\tau-1$ and $d(v)\le\lfloor(s+8)/2\rfloor$. Proof: $H-v\in C$ has $N-1$ vertices and $m-d(v)$ edges, so $11\,\tau(H-v)\le3N-3+2m-2d(v)=11\tau+s-3-2d(v)<11\tau$; hence $\tau(H-v)\le\tau-1$, so $=\tau-1$ (0.1), and $11\tau-11\le11\tau+s-3-2d(v)$ gives $2d(v)\le s+8$. (Each application below writes the inequality out.)
0.5 (Enumeration and transfer.) `enum_conn N M T0 D` outputs a labelled copy of every connected linear 3-graph with $N$ vertices, $M$ edges, $\tau\ge T_0$, $\Delta\le D$, and nothing else (L007 Lemma D). If $G\cong G_0$, every extension of $G$ by a new vertex with three edges whose pairs are pairwise disjoint, not inside an edge of $G$, with endpoints of $G$-degree $\le2$, is isomorphic to such an extension of $G_0$, which `ext_indep4`/`ext_vertex2` generate; $\tau$, connectivity and membership in $C$ are isomorphism invariants (L008 Step 5, L009 0.5).
