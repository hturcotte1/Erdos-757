## Statement

$C$ denotes the class $O^+$ of L007 and L008: 3-graphs that are (P1) linear, (P2) $F_7$-free and (P3) satisfy $e(S)\le|S|-2$ for every vertex set $S$ with $|S|\ge2$. It contains the class $O$ of ordered midpoint patterns, hence the AP pattern of every (4,5)-set. $s(H)=3n+2m-11\tau(H)$ is the I11 slack.

**Lemma 17.** Every $H\in C$ with $(n,m)=(17,12)$ has $\tau(H)\le6$.

**Lemma R (rebuild).** Let $T\in C$ be connected with $\Delta(T)\le3$ and $(n,m,\tau)=(17,13,7)$ [resp. $(14,12,6)$]. Then $T$ has a vertex of degree 3, and for every vertex $w$ of degree 3 the graph $T-w$ lies in $C$, has $(n,m,\tau)=(16,10,6)$ [resp. $(13,9,5)$], slack 2, maximum degree $\le3$, and its components are of one of the types $[(16,10,6)]$, $[(13,9,5),(3,1,1)]$, $[(10,8,4),(3,1,1),(3,1,1)]$, $[(10,7,4),(6,3,2)]$, $[(8,5,3),(8,5,3)]$ [resp. $[(13,9,5)]$, $[(10,8,4),(3,1,1)]$]; the three edges of $T$ at $w$ are $\{w,c_j,d_j\}$ with the pairs $\{c_j,d_j\}$ pairwise disjoint, none inside an edge of $T-w$, and every $c_j,d_j$ of degree $\le2$ in $T-w$.

**Theorem 1 (abstract).** Every $H\in C$ with $n=18$ vertices has $\tau(H)\le7$, i.e. $\alpha(H)\ge11$. (By (P3) $m\le16$; equivalently there is no $H\in C$ with $(n,\tau)=(18,8)$, and by Henning–Yeo such an $H$ would have $m=16$.)

**Theorem 2.** Every 18-point (4,5)-set has $h\ge11$, and the 18-point (4,5)-set $A_{18}=\{0,352,492,600,632,704,916,1200,1264,1388,1613,1696,1767,1838,2176,2476,4352,7440\}$ has $h=11$. Hence $f(18)=11$. Equivalently no 18-point (4,5)-set beats $4/7$ (that needs $h\le\lceil72/7\rceil-1=10$, with $m=16$, kb/00_START_HERE.md §4).

**Theorem 3.** Every $H\in C$ with $n\le18$ satisfies $11\,\tau(H)\le3n+2m$.

**Corollary 4.** The sizes $n\le30$ at which a set beating $4/7$ is still arithmetically possible are now $n=20,22,23,25,27,29,30$; $f(17)=10$ (known) and $f(19)\in\{11,12\}$ are unchanged by this certificate.

## Proof

### Step 0. Facts used
0.1 Additivity of $n,m,\tau,s$ over components; a single vertex has $s=3$; heredity of $C$ under induced subhypergraphs; for $H\in C$: $\tau\le m$, $\tau(H)-1\le\tau(H-v)\le\tau(H)$, $m\le n-2$ ($n\ge2$), $17\tau\le5n+3m$ (K8); a connected 3-graph with $m\ge1$ edges has $n\le2m+1$; degrees do not increase under deletion (L007 Step 0, L008 Step 0).
0.2 I11 holds on $C$ for every $n\le16$ (L008 Corollary 3; for $n\le13$ already L007 Lemma A).
0.3 (Component argument.) If I11 holds on $C$ for all graphs with fewer than $N$ vertices and $H\in C$ has $N$ vertices and $s(H)<0$, then $H$ is connected: otherwise every component has fewer than $N$ vertices, so has slack $\ge0$, and $s(H)\ge0$ by additivity.
0.4 (Exhaustive enumeration.) For a cell $(N,M,T_0)$ and a degree bound $D$, the outputs of `enum_conn N M T0 D` (and of the falsifier's `conn_dfs3`) contain a labelled copy of every connected linear 3-graph with exactly $N$ vertices, $M$ edges, $\tau\ge T_0$ and $\Delta\le D$, and nothing else (L007 Lemma D).
0.5 (Extension transfer, L007 Step 5.2, L008 Step 5.2.) If $G\cong G_0$ and $H=G+\{x,a_i,b_i\}_{i=1,2,3}$ with pairwise disjoint pairs, none inside an edge of $G$, endpoints of $G$-degree $\le2$, then $H$ is isomorphic to $G_0+\{x_0,\varphi(a_i),\varphi(b_i)\}$ for an isomorphism $\varphi$, which is one of the extensions of $G_0$ generated by `ext_indep2`/`ext_indep3`/`ext_vertex2`; isomorphic graphs have the same $\tau$, the same membership in $C$ and the same connectivity.

### Step 2. Proof of Lemma 17 (the cell (17,12,7))
2.1 Let $H\in C$, $(n,m)=(17,12)$, $\tau(H)\ge7$. K8: $17\tau\le85+36=121$, so $\tau=7$.

2.3 For $d(v)=3$, $G=H-v\in C$ has $(16,9,6)$, $s(G)=48+18-66=0$, $\Delta(G)\le3$. Its components lie in $C$, have $\le16$ vertices, hence slack $\ge0$ (0.2), so all have slack 0 and none is an isolated vertex.
